Research at FIA Operating Systems

Make things. Break things.

Join a global network of researchers, developers, and innovators building the future of autonomous systems and environmental sensing technology.

Who We Welcome

  • High Schoolers from the United States of America and the rest of the world — equal opportunity for everyone.
  • Undergraduate Students looking to gain research experience and contribute to real-world projects.
  • Graduate Students who wish to serve as advisors for our research team.

Available Positions

Select your preferred role in the application form below:

  • Advisor — Guide and mentor the research team (ideal for graduate students and professionals).
  • Research Team Member — Contribute to ongoing research projects.
  • Part-time or Full-time Researcher — Dedicate more time to in-depth research work.

How We Work

All work is fully remote. We operate as a distributed network, coordinated through Slack and Discord. This is currently a voluntary position — we are not yet an incorporated company or nonprofit organization.

In the future, most procedures will be initiated from the United States of America. Currently, we are based in Colombo, the capital of Sri Lanka.

What you'll receive: A company email domain (@fiaos.org) upon acceptance. You can also initiate any project within FIA Operating Systems — including requesting subdomains for your work — without any election procedure. We operate in an open, voted model.

Application Timeline

You will receive an acceptance or declination notification within a maximum of two weeks. Most responses are sent within 3–4 days.

If you do not receive a response after two weeks, please email n.desilva@fiaos.org.
